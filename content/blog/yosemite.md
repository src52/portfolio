For the past week, I was fortunate enough to take a "vacation" to Yosemite Valley. I put vacation in quotes because the hikes were pretty tiring, but I'm glad I did them! It's only made me want to go on tougher hikes in the future! The longest one was to North Dome, which took six and a half hours roundtrip.

Before leaving, the weather forecast showed nothing but sun for the entire five days we were scheduled to camp there. Of course, that wasn't going to happen. Mother Nature decided to show her defiance by raining and hailing on us for most of the trip! Luckily, we were still able to go on hikes to Nevada Falls and North Dome despite the inclement weather.

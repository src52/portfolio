I was fortunate enough to take a "vacation" to Yosemite Valley this past week. I put vacation in quotes because the hikes I went on were without a doubt the most strenuous, physically taxing thing I've ever done. Despite that, I don't regret it, and it's only made me want to go on tougher hikes in the future!

Before leaving, the weather forecast showed nothing but sun for the entire five days we were scheduled to camp there. Of course, that wasn't going to happen. Yosemite Valley decided to show its defiance by raining on us right as we were taking our supplies out of the car.
